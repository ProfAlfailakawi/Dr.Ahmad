**نبذة متوسطة (EN):**
Dr. Ahmad H. Alfailakawi is an Associate Professor of Educational Technology at the Public Authority for Applied Education and Training (PAAET) and an adjunct professor at Kuwait University. He holds a PhD and MA from the University of Northern Colorado. He is the author of 9 books and 18 peer-reviewed studies on educational technology, AI in education, e-learning and LMS, smart schools, gamification, and digital transformation. He advises Kuwait's Ministry of Information, the National Council for Culture, Arts and Letters, the National Library, and the Public Authority for Youth, and writes a column for Al-Jarida newspaper.
