### Long bio (~230 words)
Dr. Ahmad H. Alfailakawi is a Kuwaiti professor, author, researcher, and consultant working at the intersection of educational technology and artificial intelligence. His guiding principle — "keeping the human at the heart of the machine" — runs through more than two decades of teaching, research, authorship, and public service.

He holds a PhD in Educational Technology (with honors) from the University of Northern Colorado, where his doctoral research designed an internet-based social learning network to support high-school teaching staff. He is an associate professor at the College of Basic Education (PAAET) and adjunct faculty in the College of Education at Kuwait University.

Dr. Alfailakawi has authored 9 books — including an encyclopedia of educational technology and works on AI governance and big data, gamification, smart schools, virtual worlds, assistive technology for special needs, and digital learning — alongside 18 peer-reviewed studies on e-learning, LMS adoption, cloud computing, and augmented reality in education.

He advises several Kuwaiti national institutions, including the Ministry of Information, the National Council for Culture, Arts and Letters, the National Library of Kuwait, and the Public Authority for Youth, and is a regular columnist for Al-Jarida. A Microsoft Innovative Educator and member of ISTE, AECT, and Pi Lambda Theta, he is a frequent television commentator and workshop leader on technology, learning, and youth development across the Gulf.
